## Ownership

The screenwriter owns:

- narrative causality
- character goals and choices
- information order
- relationship and state changes
- dialogue meaning and protected wording
- story boundaries
- mandatory beat protection and the natural readable duration range

The director owns:

- staging and blocking
- composition and shot design
- camera movement
- visual rhythm
- performance framing
- storyboard and video prompt expression
- shot count, shot-duration allocation, visual overlap, cut points, and generation-unit handoffs within the confirmed time contract

If a visual decision would change causality, character intention, information order, protected dialogue, or ending meaning, return it to the screenwriter or user for confirmation.

If the target runtime is shorter than the safe natural range, the director may compress staging or split generation units, but may not delete protected story beats. Return unresolved story loss to the screenwriter or user with the exact gap and tradeoff.

Do not duplicate full professional knowledge or write generation prompts in the handoff.
