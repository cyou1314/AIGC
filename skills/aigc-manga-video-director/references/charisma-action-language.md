# Charisma And Cool Action Language

Use this reference when the user asks for a character to look `帅`, `酷`, `有压迫感`, `有气场`, `高级`, `神性`, `疯批`, `强者感`, `男主感`, `反派感`, or when a prompt risks using empty adjectives such as `handsome`, `cool`, `badass`, `cinematic`, or `霸气` without visible camera/action controls.

Core principle: `帅` is not an adjective. It is produced by silhouette, posture, timing, restraint, action economy, camera power relation, environmental reaction, and the way the shot withholds or releases force.

When the user asks to learn from cool videos, action references, hype edits, fight scenes, anime bursts, or a specific `燃/炫/高燃剪辑/动作高级` feeling, also read `cool-video-reference-patterns.md`. Choose one reusable pattern there before writing character-level charisma details here.

## The Charisma Formula

Translate `帅` into these layers:

1. **Silhouette**: readable outline, head/shoulder/weapon/cloak/hair shape, clean negative space around the body.
2. **Posture**: stable center of gravity, relaxed shoulders, controlled spine, chin/eye line, weight shift.
3. **Anticipation**: a half-second pause before action, breath stop, fingers tightening, eyes moving before the body.
4. **Action economy**: one precise action, no flailing, no excessive gestures, clear start and endpoint.
5. **Camera power**: low angle, over-shoulder dominance, slow push-in, orbit, locked frame, or reveal based on the character's status.
6. **Rhythm**: slow before fast, fast impact then stillness, or delayed reaction after the character acts.
7. **Environmental response**: coat hem, hair, dust, smoke, sparks, rain, loose paper, shadow, or light responds to the action.
8. **Sound**: cloth snap, weapon ring, footstep, breath, silence before impact, low room tone, impact hit.
9. **Endpoint**: finish on a controlled pose, eye contact, weapon held still, opponent/background reacting, or the character not needing to look back.

If a prompt says `帅`, replace it with at least four of these layers.

## Common Types Of Coolness

### Calm Dominance

Use for powerful protagonists, gods, senior warriors, controlled villains.

- Body: minimal movement, shoulders relaxed, one hand resting on weapon/prop.
- Eyes: gaze moves first; face barely reacts.
- Camera: low-angle medium shot, slow dolly-in, locked frame, or slight orbit.
- Rhythm: pause -> one clean action -> still endpoint.
- Environment: cloak/hair moves after the body, dust or light shifts subtly.

Prompt pattern:

`He stands still while the room moves around him; only his eyes shift toward the threat. After a half-second pause, his right hand lowers to the sword hilt, thumb pushing the guard open by two centimeters. Camera holds a low-angle medium shot and slowly pushes in; cloak edge and loose dust drift behind him. End on his calm side profile, weapon barely unsheathed, no exaggerated expression.`

### Violent Precision

Use for fast attacks, assassins, battle cuts, sharp reveal moments.

- Body: compressed stance, hips/shoulders aligned before release.
- Action: one decisive slash, step, turn, grab, or dodge.
- Camera: whip pan only if motivated; otherwise track the action with a clean endpoint.
- Rhythm: stillness -> explosive motion -> hard stop.
- Environment: impact line, sparks, cloth snap, opponent reaction, ground dust.

Prompt pattern:

`Start in a silent close three-quarter frame. His body stays compact, left foot sliding half a step forward before the strike. In one clean motion he draws the blade across the foreground; camera tracks the blade for a short lateral burst then stops as he freezes in profile. Sparks and torn cloth lag behind the motion. End with the blade held steady, opponent blurred behind him reacting one beat late.`

### Elegant Cool

Use for noble, immortal, beautiful, or refined characters.

- Body: long lines, wrist/neck/head angle, restrained gesture.
- Camera: slow crane, orbit, or drifting side track.
- Rhythm: continuous, smooth, no sudden shake.
- Environment: sleeves, hair, petals, snow, mist, light ribbon.
- Endpoint: composed gaze or small expression change.

Prompt pattern:

`He turns only his wrist, letting the sleeve arc through the frame before his body follows. Camera performs a slow left-to-right orbit at chest height, keeping his face in clean three-quarter profile. Hair and sleeve trail in delayed motion, pale light sliding across the cheekbone. End on a composed downward glance, the gesture finished before the fabric settles.`

### Arrogant / Dangerous Cool

Use for antagonists, antiheroes, high-status enemies.

- Body: takes space, leans back or looks down, does not rush.
- Eyes: looks past the opponent before acknowledging them.
- Camera: low angle from opponent side, foreground victim/weapon blur.
- Rhythm: delayed response, small smile, slow step.
- Environment: shadow crosses face, light isolates eyes, room tone drops.

Prompt pattern:

`He does not turn immediately. The camera starts behind the opponent's shoulder, low and slightly tilted upward, making him dominate the doorway. He slowly raises his eyes first, then turns his head just enough to acknowledge the threat. A faint smile appears and disappears. Background sound drops; his shadow stretches across the floor. End with him still relaxed while the opponent remains tense in the foreground blur.`

### Tragic Hero Cool

Use when `帅` should carry pain, burden, sacrifice, or restraint.

- Body: still heroic silhouette, but slightly damaged or tired.
- Action: small controlled recovery, not triumphant posing.
- Camera: slow push-in or pull-back that isolates him.
- Rhythm: action finishes, then emotion leaks through a micro-expression.
- Environment: rain, ash, torn cloth, flickering light, distant sound.

Prompt pattern:

`He stands after the impact, coat torn and shoulders steady despite exhaustion. Camera slowly pulls back from a tight close-up to reveal the empty battlefield around him. His hand trembles once, then tightens around the broken weapon. Rain slides across his face; he does not wipe it away. End on a lonely full-body silhouette, head lowered but spine unbroken.`

## Camera Rules For Making A Character Look Cool

Choose the camera based on the type of charisma:

| Desired effect | Camera choice | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Powerful / godlike | low-angle medium or full-body shot | makes silhouette and vertical lines dominate |
| Controlled / dangerous | locked frame or slow push-in | shows the character does not need motion to create pressure |
| Fast / lethal | short tracking burst with hard endpoint | makes action precise instead of chaotic |
| Elegant | slow orbit or side drift | shows line, sleeve, hair, and face profile |
| Mysterious | partial occlusion, backlight, delayed reveal | creates withholding before recognition |
| Lone hero | slow pull-back after action | turns the figure into a symbolic silhouette |
| Arrogant | opponent POV or over-shoulder low angle | makes power relation visible |

Avoid:

- random shaky camera for coolness
- too many camera moves in one short clip
- close-up only without body silhouette
- full-body shot with no action endpoint
- `360 orbit` unless the body posture and environment can support it

## Action Rules For Making A Character Look Cool

Cool action is usually precise, delayed, and economical.

Use:

- one main action per shot
- a visible preparation beat
- a clean endpoint pose
- delayed secondary motion: sleeve, hair, cloak, dust, sparks
- reaction from the world or opponent after the action
- hands moving with purpose, not random gesturing

Avoid:

- several attacks in one short prompt
- fast action with no start pose
- spinning, jumping, slashing, turning, and shouting all in one clip
- facial expression changing too much during complex movement
- vague verbs such as `fights coolly`, `moves handsomely`, `poses stylishly`

## Prompt Rewrite Rules

Replace weak wording with visible controls:

| Weak phrase | Better direction |
|---|---|
| `他帅气地拔剑` | `he pauses, thumb pushes the guard open, blade slides out two centimeters before the full draw` |
| `霸气登场` | `low-angle doorway silhouette, coat hem moving before face reveal, room tone drops` |
| `很酷地转身` | `eyes shift first, shoulders stay relaxed, head turns last, camera holds profile` |
| `帅气打斗` | `one clean dodge, one counterstrike, hard stop on controlled stance` |
| `男主感` | `stable center, restrained expression, foreground reacts around him, camera gives him negative space` |
| `神性` | `upright stillness, backlight halo edge, slow camera rise, minimal human reaction` |
| `疯批感` | `calm smile under violent context, tiny delayed eye movement, sudden precise action, silence after impact` |

## Seedance Prompt Checklist

Before final output, check:

- If the user wants a video-reference feel, did you choose one pattern from `cool-video-reference-patterns.md` first?
- Does the prompt show the silhouette?
- Is there a preparation beat before the action?
- Is the main action singular and readable?
- Does the camera create power, elegance, mystery, danger, or loneliness?
- Does the environment react after the character moves?
- Is the endpoint a strong held image?
- Did the prompt avoid empty words like `帅`, `酷`, `霸气`, `高级` unless they are supported by visible details?

If not, rewrite before delivery.
